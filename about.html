Our Methodology

The Trimjourney Approach

Step 1

Problem Definition

We start by precisely defining the business challenge. Vague problems get vague answers. A focused problem statement unlocks targeted, useful guidance.

Step 2

Value Stream Mapping

Our agents map your current process to identify where value is being lost — whether through waste, inefficiency, compliance gaps, or missed revenue opportunities.

Step 3

Targeted Analysis

Using industry benchmarks and Lean AI classification, we assess which automation or process changes will deliver the highest ROI for your specific situation.

Step 4

Actionable Recommendation

You receive a clear, prioritized action plan — not a report to file away. Each recommendation includes the steps, resources, and expected outcome.

Step 5

Feasibility Assessment

Every plan is assessed for cost-effectiveness and practical implementation in your business context — no recommendations that look good on paper but fail in the real world.
