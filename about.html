Our Story

Built by Operators, Led by Advisors

We started Apex because we were tired of consultants who gave advice without accountability. We built a firm where expertise meets execution.

Founded in 2008

Our Mission

Apex Consulting was founded on a simple but powerful belief: great advice without great execution is worthless.

Our founders came from operations, finance, and technology — not just advisory. They saw firsthand how top-tier strategy recommendations gathered dust because the execution gap was never bridged. So they built a consulting firm that closes that gap.

Today, with 180+ consultants across four global offices, we serve businesses from pre-revenue startups to multi-billion-dollar enterprises — always with the same philosophy: we don't leave until it works.

Our Journey

Milestones That Define Us

2008

Founded in New York

Three founders, a shared office, and a mission to make consulting accountable. First client: a healthcare startup that needed a turnaround.

2012

London Office Opens

Expanded to Europe with 25 consultants. Added Financial Advisory practice to serve growing demand from private equity clients.

2016

Digital Transformation Practice Launched

Responded to the cloud revolution by building a dedicated digital practice. Helped 40 enterprises migrate to cloud in 24 months.

2019
